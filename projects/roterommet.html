roterommet

Jazz Trio · Oslo

roterommet is a jazz trio I play in together with two close friends, both fellow astrophysics students. Alongside orbits and perturbation equations, we spend our evenings navigating a different kind of dynamical system — one built on rhythm, harmony, and improvisation.

The name is an homage to J. O. Øgrim and H. Ormestad.

Details

  • Trio format, formed among physics students in Oslo
  • Repertoire rooted in the jazz standard tradition
  • Regular rehearsals and informal performances
